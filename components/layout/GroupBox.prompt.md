The grouping primitive for a J-Runner window — everything on MainForm lives inside one. Caption is muted, not bold.

```jsx
<GroupBox title="Glitch Chip Programming">
  <Radio checked label="USB" /><Radio label="LPT" />
</GroupBox>
```

There are no cards in this product: no shadow, no fill contrast beyond PanelBg, no accent left border. A group is a hairline and a caption.
