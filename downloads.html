Where to unzip it

Somewhere short, permanent and free of spaces or non-English characters — C:\JRunner\ works well. J-Runner writes dumps, built images and logs into folders alongside itself, so running it from inside the zip or from a Downloads folder will cause problems later.

Flasher firmware

J-Runner talks to the hardware; the hardware needs its own firmware first. Pick the one that matches the board you own.

PicoFlasherRaspberry Pi Pico / RP2040 — the standard choice→ Pico2FlasherRaspberry Pi Pico 2 / RP2350→ picoflasher (clone build)RP2040 clones with exactly 20 GPIO headers per side→ Buy a Raspberry Pi PicoIf you don't own one yet→

Glitch chip firmware

You do not need to download this separately — J-Runner flashes it for you from Advanced → Program Glitch Chip. Hold BOOTSEL while plugging the Pico in, choose RPicoRGH, and click Program.
